# AI Product Notes

A personal knowledge base for AI product thinking, product management learning, and AI-native workflow exploration.

This repository records my notes, reflections, frameworks, and research related to AI products, product roles, Vibe Coding, and practical AI tool usage.

---

## About This Repository

`ai-product-notes` is used to organize my learning process around AI products and internet product roles.

It is not only a note archive, but also a portfolio-oriented space that shows how I think about products, users, workflows, and AI-assisted building.

The repository may include:

- AI product case studies
- Product manager learning notes
- Product operation notes
- AI tool workflow records
- Vibe Coding methodology
- Internship preparation materials
- JD analysis and role research
- Product teardown notes
- Personal project reflections

---

## Why I Built This

I am interested in AI product roles and want to better understand how real AI products are designed, positioned, evaluated, and improved.

Instead of keeping scattered notes across different apps and documents, I use this repository to turn learning into structured, reusable, and reviewable materials.

The long-term goal is to connect three things:

1. Product thinking
2. AI tool practice
3. Real project building
